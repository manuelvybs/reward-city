# Reward City — Step 4 (colour wash, unlock moment, finale, end card) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Colour comes back like wet paint (spreading up the buildings as well as along the street, with darker watercolour edges); unlocking a card feels like a reward; the finale floods the city; the end card carries the VYBS logo.

**Architecture:** The visual colour field becomes 2-D: `ColourField` in `paint.js` keeps paint *sources* (centre, radius, start, grow time) and redraws a 520×100 canvas (0.5 m per pixel over x −30…230, y −10…40) with soft radial blobs while any source is still growing; the shader samples it by world (x, y). `game.colourAt(x)` stays the gameplay/test truth (sky tint, tests). New `fx.js` draws instanced particles (sparkle, confetti, dust) and the finale's falling gift cards. `main.js` turns game events into sources, effects and camera moves.

**Tech Stack:** three.js, Canvas 2D for the field and particle sprites.
