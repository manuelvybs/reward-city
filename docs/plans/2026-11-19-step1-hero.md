# Reward City — Step 1 (hero, phone, coin beam) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** The VYBS astronaut, side-on like the game camera, walks and jumps holding his phone, beams a stream of gold coins at a target while the coin meter drains, and plays at a kiosk while it refills. Shown in `tools/hero-viewer.html` for Manuel's check-in.

**Architecture:** Pure logic in Node-testable modules (`coinsim.js`: meter, coin launch/flight/hits). Rendering split into `phone.js` (built in code, VYBS feed screen), `hero.js` (GLB, clips, both hands on the phone with world-space two-bone IK, three phone poses blended: carry / beam / play), `coins.js` (instanced gold coins). The viewer glues them on a plain floor with a placeholder kiosk and a placeholder locked card.

**Tech Stack:** three.js 0.187.0 (vendored, import map), ES modules, `node:test`, Playwright 1.61.1 screenshots.

## Numbers

- Hero 1.2 m tall; feet at y=0; faces +Z in the GLB, so facing right = yaw +π/2 (turned 0.35 rad toward the camera).
- Phone 0.16 × 0.30 × 0.02 m. Grips at its left/right edges. Poses relative to the hero root (x forward, y up): carry (0.20, 0.55) screen tilted up 0.5 rad; beam (0.34, 0.72) screen facing forward; play (0.22, 0.82) screen facing the visor.
- Coins: spout = top of the phone; launch speed 4.2 m/s at 35°±8° in the facing direction, ±0.25 m/s jitter, gravity 9.8; disc r 0.055 m; life 2.2 s; land and fade on the floor.
- Meter 0–100. Beaming drains 17/s and emits 30 coins/s while above 0. Playing refills 55/s.
