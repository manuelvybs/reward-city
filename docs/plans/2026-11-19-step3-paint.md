# Reward City — Step 3 (painted art) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Replace the greybox with a soft painted crumbling town in our own style, drawn in VYBS colour and shown grey until colour comes back (the wash shader itself is step 4; this step ships the art and a simple grey↔colour mix).

**Architecture:** Every image is generated with `fal-ai/nano-banana-pro` via `tools/fal.mjs` (logged to `tools/fal-log.jsonl`), prompts in `tools/prompts/paint-*.json`, raw outputs in `art-src/paint/`. Cut-outs are painted on flat white and keyed to alpha (`tools/key-white.mjs`, flood fill from the border so whites inside a building survive); textures made tileable (`tools/tileable.mjs`). Shipped as webp in `assets/paint/`. `src/paint.js` loads them and gives `paintMaterial(tex, opts)`: a sprite shader that shows the art desaturated + warm-grey, mixing to full colour by `uColour` (per sprite for now, the field in step 4).

**Tech Stack:** fal Nano Banana Pro, ImageMagick (`magick`), three.js.

---

## Our town (not Rust Bloom's)

A crumbling **modern** town, not old-European ruins: shuttered shopfronts and corner stores, a dead arcade hall with an unlit sign, empty billboard and neon sign frames, a water tower, tram poles and sagging wires, scaffolding, rubble and cracked paving, morning mist. Colour palette when lit: VYBS indigo `#3A43C4`, `#5B69FE`, lavender `#A8B1E7`, gold `#FFD401`, warm window light.

## As built (2026-11-19)

- Concepts A/B/C → Manuel picked **B (watercolour + ink line)**. 12 images in all on `fal-ai/nano-banana-pro(/edit)` with concept B as the style reference (≈ $2.25 incl. concepts); log in `tools/fal-log.jsonl`.
- `tools/cutout.mjs`: paper flood-filled from the border (+ optional `holes` pass for enclosed paper: water-tower legs, sign frames), building sheets sliced at column gaps above the shared ink baseline (lowest heavily inked row), props by 8-connected blobs, ground/wall made tileable by crossfade. Gotcha: sharp returns a blurred 1-channel raw buffer as 3 channels, read with `info.channels` stride. `tools/preview-sprites.mjs` checks alpha on a coloured board (ffmpeg mangles transparent webp).
- 32 sprites + ground/wall/sky = 2.1 MB in `assets/paint/` with `manifest.json` (metres per sprite).
- `src/paint.js`: one shader for every painted thing, grey (warm, lifted) ↔ colour by a 1-D field from `game.colourAt(x)` with a noisy wet-paint edge; ground pieces = painted street strip over stone; sky shader. No shadow maps: a blob shadow under the hero.
- Rows: near z −8.5 ×0.66 (climbs onto the ledge), mid z −21 ×0.62, far z −40 ×0.55, so sky shows above the roofs.
- `npm test` 29/29; playtest 0 errors (local server drops a parallel request now and then; only never-loaded URLs count); `art-src/step3-playthrough.mp4`.
